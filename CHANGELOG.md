# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version. The record player's music screen gets a Radio tab with internet radio stations from Radio Browser: popular stations, stations in China, stations in Chinese, search and favorites.
- Paste a stream link to play any station, including .pls, .m3u and .m3u8 links.
- The My music tab plays the songs in a folder on your PC.
- The radio plays instead of the game's music, needs power like a record, and gives the same morale as a record.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.21671.
